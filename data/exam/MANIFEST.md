# Exam papers — manifest

Past papers, mark schemes and examiner reports for GCSE Physics (8463)
Paper 1, Foundation (1F) and Higher (1H). The PDFs (`data/exam/src/`), their
extracted text (`data/exam/txt/`) and the outcome notes made from them
(`data/exam/electricity-outcomes.md`) stay local and gitignored: the repo is
public and these documents are copyright. Only this manifest is committed.

Downloaded 2026-10-24 (brief docs/briefs/2026-10-19-evidence-layer.md, step 1a).
41 files from the exam board, 6 from physicsandmathstutor.com.

The exam board links were taken from the exam board's Physics 8463
"Assessment resources" page (its full search listing of 216 resources), not
guessed. Every file from the exam board matched the SHA-1 the board lists
for it. Modified (large-print) papers and inserts were not downloaded. The
six physicsandmathstutor.com files are the exam board's own documents
re-hosted there, because the exam board no longer lists them. Text was
extracted page by page with Python 3.14.3 and pypdf.
