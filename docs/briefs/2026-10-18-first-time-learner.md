2. Tutor behaviour, in the shared teach code, behind one flag
   (FIRST_TIME, on by default; with it off, prompts are byte-identical
   to now, proved with the prompt-building comparison):
   a. The lesson opens by finding out what she already knows: one short
      question about the everyday idea behind the lesson (never yes/no,
      never using a term the lesson is about to teach). Her answer sets
      where the tutor starts.
   b. At most one new term or idea per tutor message. The first time a
      term is used it is explained in everyday words, with an everyday
      comparison where one helps. No term is used before it has been
      explained in this lesson, unless she has shown she knows it.
   c. Build from the concrete to the abstract: what you would see or
      measure first, the name for it second, the rule or graph third.
   d. Never ask a question whose answer appears in the same message or
      the one before it. Questions make her use or apply what was
      taught, not repeat it.
   e. If she says she does not know a basic idea, teach it from the
      lesson's basics (step 3) before going on.
   f. When the key point being taught has a diagram, show it in the
      conversation at that moment (step 4) and refer to it in words.
   g. Keep the existing fixes (fix23, fix25, fixT4, FLOW_FIX, Sonnet).
