## Key point 3: Force and change in momentum

25. [3.1] Tutor: A resultant force on an object changes its velocity, so it changes its momentum. Momentum after minus momentum before is called the change in momentum, written m Δv (Δ means "change in").
   Button: Next

26. [3.2] Tutor: Putting F = m a together with a = Δv ÷ Δt gives F = m Δv ÷ Δt.
   Button: Next

27. [3.2b] Tutor: In words: force = change in momentum ÷ time taken. F is in newtons (N), m Δv in kg m/s, and Δt, the time taken, in seconds (s).
   Button: Next

28. [3.3] Tutor: Force equals the rate of change of momentum: how much the momentum changes each second. The same change in momentum spread over a longer time needs a smaller force.
   Button: Next
