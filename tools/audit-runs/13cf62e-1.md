## Findings

### F1. Diagram: the ammeter loop's join to the test wire is hidden, so the circuit reads wrongly (medium)
- File: diagrams/physics/p2-wire-length.svg (new; used by keyPoints[2].diagram)
- Old text: (new file)
- New text: line 5 path d="M110 250 H80 V100 H400 V250 H330"; line 10 test wire line x1=110 to x2=370 at y=250, stroke #4ecfaa; line 16 clip circle at cx=330 cy=250.
- Fault: the return lead of the main loop runs along y=250 from x=400 to x=330. That is on top of the test wire, which runs from x=110 to x=370. The test wire is drawn after the lead, so the lead is hidden from x=370 to x=330. On screen, the white lead seems to meet the test wire at its right-hand end (x=370), not at the moving clip (x=330). So the picture suggests the current flows through the whole wire, with only the voltmeter on the moving clip. That is not what the aria-label says ("a voltmeter in parallel across the length of wire between two clips, one clip moved to change the length"). It also does not match keyPoints[2].content, where moving the clip sets the length that carries the current. The ammeter in series and the voltmeter in parallel between the two clips are both correct.
- Evidence: SVG lines 1, 5, 10, 16. To fix, bring the lead down to the clip at x=330 from above or below, instead of running it along the wire.

### F4. keyPoints[1] treats the push from the cell as the potential difference across the component (medium, contradiction)
- File: keyPoints[1].content, paragraph 2
- Old text: (no equivalent; old said "The greater the resistance, the less current flows for a given potential difference.")
- New text: "The first thing is the push from the cell: the potential difference across the component."
- Fault: this sentence makes "the push from the cell" the same as "the potential difference across the component". They are only equal when the component is alone with the cell. The paragraph just before uses a lamp and a resistor together in one loop. There the cell's potential difference is shared, so the potential difference across the lamp is less than the push from the cell. This contradicts p2-series-parallel keyPoints[0]: "the total potential difference of the supply is shared between the components".
- Evidence: data/spec/physics.md line 331 (current depends on "the potential difference (V) across the component") and line 416; lessons/physics/p2-series-parallel.json keyPoints[0].content.
