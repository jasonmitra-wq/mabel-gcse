## Findings

### F1. Fact added, and it is false: "a later lesson explains" the fuse (HIGH)
- File: lessons/physics/p2-components.json
- Field: keyPoints[0].content (paragraph 3). The same text was copied into data/glossary/physics.json as the "fuse" definition.
- Old: "The chart also shows a fuse: a rectangle with a line through it."
- New: "The chart also shows a <dfn>fuse</dfn>, a safety part that a later lesson explains: a rectangle with a line through it."
- Fault: false fact, above-spec content brought back, and a reversal of a decision already made.
  - No physics lesson explains fuses. Fuses were cut from p2-domestic and p2-power-energy ("Fuse hold released", P2-FIX-LOG.md lines 441-465; line 731: "Jason decision P2 B1/A1 ... fuses stay cut").
  - The spec does not mention fuses. In data/spec/physics.md the only match for "fuse" is "diffuse reflection" (line 1991).
  - The standing rule in P2-TRIAGE.md says above-spec content is "removed, never signposted". This sentence signposts it.
  - It also reverses step-d fix F5 (P2-FIX-LOG.md line 810), which says: "fuse kept as a symbol only ... its function, key term and <dfn> removed". The new text puts the <dfn> and a function ("safety part") back.
  - The build produced a new glossary entry. Its def is the whole lesson sentence, starting "The chart also shows a fuse ...". So the false claim now appears in the glossary too.
- Evidence: grep -il fuse lessons/physics/*.json finds only p2-components plus non-electrical matches (p4, p6, p7, e.g. "diffuse"). See also the P2-FIX-LOG.md lines above.
- Note: the fuse is only a picture label (keyPoints[0].pictures[1].labels), so it needs to count as "explained". A neutral wording would do that without claiming a later lesson. Example: "The chart also shows the symbol for a fuse: a rectangle with a line through it", with a glossary entry that describes only the symbol. That fix is for the caller to decide.

- Field: keyPoints[3].content (last sentence).
