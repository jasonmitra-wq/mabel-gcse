4. [own knowledge] File: lessons/physics/p1-energy-stores.json
   Field: keyPoints[1].keyTerms[2].def and keyPoints[1].content
   Fault: Not an error, but a possible point of confusion. Infrared radiation also carries energy from a hotter object to a cooler one, for example the Sun or a fire warming you. Many GCSE texts call this heating by radiation. With heating defined as "from hotter to cooler... always", a student may ask why radiation is not heating. The text follows TRIAGE fact3 and fact4, so no change is needed. A tutor_note could cover it if testers raise it again.
