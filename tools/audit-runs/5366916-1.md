# Audit of G5 lesson rewrite against 5366916 (run 1)

Changed lessons: lessons/geography/g5-development.json, g5-nigeria.json, g5-uk-economy.json (uncommitted), plus text edits in diagrams/geography/g5-brandt-line.svg and g5-nigeria-context.svg.
No tools/spec-check-results/G5-TRIAGE.md exists, so content-fix mode does not apply. The check-rewrite FAILs below follow from the intended rewrite (brief, G5-FIX-LOG.md, tools/pilot/g5-figures.md). Each change was judged on its merits.

## Findings (most important first)

1. g5-nigeria.json, keyPoints[2].content, tables[0].title, checkpoints.2.question. New: "Shell, an oil company based in the UK, has taken oil from the Niger Delta for decades." Table: "Advantages and disadvantages of Shell's oil operations for Nigeria". Old: "Shell has operated oil extraction in the Niger Delta since 1958." Fault: factual error that was there before the change and is still there. Shell sold its onshore Niger Delta subsidiary (SPDC) to Renaissance in March 2025. The lesson presents Shell's onshore operation as current. Evidence: [own knowledge].

2. g5-development.json, sources[0]. Figure text: "Kenya: international tourist arrivals about 1.18 million in 2015 ...". URL: NY.GNP.PCAP.CD for USA, GBR, KEN, NGA, NER. Fault: the source entry is mislabelled. keyPoints[0].content uses "$88,810 in the USA ... $750 in Niger" (2025), but no figure field in this lesson's sources lists the GNI values for the USA, UK, Nigeria or Niger. They appear only in tools/pilot/g5-figures.md. The Kenya figure is listed twice (sources[0] and sources[6]).

3. g5-development.json, examTips[3]. New: "Learn the difference: the range is highest minus lowest; the interquartile range is the upper quartile minus the lower quartile." Fault: this is a statistics skill with no link to development. "Quartile" is never taught in the lesson, so a first-time learner cannot use the tip. It belongs in a skills lesson. G5-FIX-LOG says it is justified by E88 (June 2023 02.8), but that does not explain why it sits here.

4. g5-development.json, keyPoints[4].content. New: "Aid is money, goods or skills given by one country or charity to another." Fault: contradiction. The same lesson's keyTerms Aid ("given or lent on very easy terms") and commonMistakes[3] ("gifts, or loans on very easy terms") disagree with it, and so do g5-nigeria keyPoints[3]. The B2 fix ("aid includes concessional loans") was not carried into the content.

5. diagrams/geography/g5-development-gap-causes.svg (used by g5-development keyPoints[3]). Text: "Four causes, one outcome": Historical, Trade, Debt, Physical. The lesson now says "Its causes come in three groups" (physical, economic, historical), and so do introTips[2], memTip and examTips[1]. Fault: the diagram contradicts the lesson.

6. g5-development.json, keyPoints[4].content: "In Kenya, a poorer, lower-middle-income country". Fault: the specification asks for tourism "in an LIC or NEE" (data/spec/geography.md 3.2.2.2). The lesson's classification point teaches only "HICs, middle income countries, and LICs", and its NEE examples leave out Kenya. "Lower-middle-income" is never explained, and the learner is not told that Kenya is an acceptable LIC or NEE example. The old UMIC and LMIC categories were removed but the term is still used here.

7. g5-uk-economy.json, keyPoints[0].content: "From the 1980s, governments stopped protecting loss-making industries". Fault: this date is not in the sources field. g5-figures.md says "every dated UK policy event" was cut. intro "A hundred years ago" is a time claim that is also unsourced.

8. g5-nigeria.json, keyPoints[1]. Fault: the specification's "balance between different sectors of the economy" (3.2.2.3) is now given only as fuel's share of exports. No sourced share of GDP or jobs by sector remains, so the learner has no evidence for the balance. The old sector figures were cut as unsourced, and none were sourced to replace them.

9. g5-nigeria.json, keyPoints[0].content and keyTerms ECOWAS: "a group of West African countries that trade freely with each other". Fault: this overstates ECOWAS. Trade inside ECOWAS still faces barriers, and Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger left in 2025. [own knowledge]

10. Unsourced quantities in diagrams: g5-nigeria-context.svg "Conflict has displaced millions", and g5-uk-sector-shift.svg dates "1900", "1960". These are outside the sources fields.

11. g5-uk-economy.json, keyPoints[4].content: "motorways are widened and use the hard shoulder at busy times". Fault: this is out of date. New all-lane-running schemes were stopped in 2023. [own knowledge]

12. Plain-English rules (tools/plain-english-rules.md): many sentences are over 20 words. Examples: g5-development keyPoints[1].content (40 words, the World Bank sentence), and g5-nigeria and g5-uk intro (36 words each). Undefined terms: "royalties" (g5-nigeria-oil-balance.svg), and "quaternary" in g5-uk keyPoints[1], which is introduced without primary, secondary or tertiary in that lesson. The Nigeria lesson teaches those sectors earlier, so this is minor.

Specification coverage: every bullet of 3.2.2.1 to 3.2.2.4 is now addressed. The only weak point is finding 8.

Nothing needed was lost, apart from findings 6 and 8. Political causes, the debt cycle, HIPC by name, Bodo Creek and Northern Powerhouse were cut, but none of them is required by the specification.
