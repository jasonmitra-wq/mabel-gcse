## Findings

### 1. [low] "still" in the new option 0 and both feedbacks hints at a past state that keyPoints[4] does not give
- File: lessons/geography/g5-nigeria.json
- Field: checkpoints["4"].options[0], .correctFeedback, .wrongFeedback
- Old: "Life expectancy has risen, but the north has gained less than the south"
- New: "Life expectancy has risen, but the north is still poorer than the south"
- Fault: "still" implies the north was also poorer before. keyPoints[4] gives only present levels; it does not
  say the north was poorer in 2000. This is a much smaller version of the change claim the fix removed. It is
  probably acceptable: keyPoints[4] says "many people remain poor", and the context paragraph in keyPoints[0]
  describes a long-standing north-south contrast. Dropping "still" ("the north is poorer than the south") would
  remove the implied claim.
- Evidence: keyPoints[4].content: "But the gains are uneven. The south, with the oil and Lagos, is richer. The north
  has more poverty, fewer children in school..."
