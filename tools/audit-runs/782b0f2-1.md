## Findings

### Finding 1 (low): sentence over the length limit
- File: lessons/geography/g5-nigeria.script.json
- Field: keyPoints[4].steps (id 5.8).idk
- Old: "No problem. A good line would be: development has helped Nigerians live longer, but the gains are uneven, with the north gaining less than the south."
- New: "No problem. A good line would be: development has helped Nigerians live longer, but the gains are uneven, and the north is poorer than the south."
- Fault: The second sentence is 24 words. That breaks the 20-word limit in the brief and tools/plain-english-rules.md ("never more than 15"). The old line was also 24 words, so the length is not new, but the line was rewritten without fixing it. The ", and" joins two clauses that could be split.
- Evidence: Word count of "A good line would be: ... than the south." is 24. A possible split: "...but the gains are uneven. The north is poorer than the south." This is a model answer, so it is less important than slide text.

### Finding 2 (low): dropping "only" weakens the reasoning
- File: lessons/geography/g5-nigeria.json
- Field: keyPoints[4].content (last sentence)
- Old: "Nigeria's GNI per head is still only $1,360, so many people remain poor."
- New: "In 2025 Nigeria's GNI per head was $1,360, so many people are still poor."
- Fault: No fact is lost. But "only" was the word that told the reader $1,360 is low, and the "so" depends on that. keyPoints[4] gives no comparison figure. The UK figure of $54,550 appears only in g5-development and script step 1.3, not in keyPoints[0] or [4]. A learner reading keyPoints[4] alone gets a figure and a "so" with no reason why the figure means poverty. "only" could be kept without a claim about change: "In 2025 Nigeria's GNI per head was only $1,360, so many people are still poor." (15 words.)
- Evidence: Old and new text above. Script step 1.3 keeps "only $1,360 ... compared with $54,550 in the UK".

### Finding 3 (low, existed before, outside the changed lines): $1,360 still has no year in the script
- File: lessons/geography/g5-nigeria.script.json
- Field: keyPoints[0].steps (id 1.3).text
- Old and new (unchanged): "...its GNI per head is only $1,360 a year, compared with $54,550 in the UK. So many Nigerians are still poor."
- Fault: The brief says its purpose is to "give $1,360 its year". It is now dated in the slides (content and writeBullets) but not in the script, which is what she hears. The year is 2025 for both figures (sources[2]; the g5-development keyPoint says "In 2025 it was about $88,810 in the USA, $54,550 in the UK, $1,360 in Nigeria").
- Evidence: Line 87 of the script file.
