## C. Faults that were there before the change (not part of this diff)

C1. p5-gravity keyPoints[1].content: "Near Earth’s surface, the field is roughly uniform (the same in all directions near the surface)." This definition of uniform is wrong. A uniform field has the same strength and direction everywhere in the region. Near the surface the field points down, not "in all directions". [own knowledge]

C2. p5-gravity keyPoints[2].content: "Too fast, and the object escapes." This overstates it. A little too fast gives a higher or elliptical orbit. Escape needs much more speed. Spec 4.8.1.3 says only "for a stable orbit, the radius must change if the speed changes." [own knowledge for escape]

C3. p5-gravity revisionCardBullets[8]: "Geostationary satellites: 24-hour period, same position above Earth". No key point teaches geostationary satellites, so this fact appears only on the revision card.

C4. p5-gravity checkpoints."1".options[1] is about 110 characters and the others about 60-75. The right answer is given away by its length.

C5. p5-momentum intro: "Momentum is always conserved in collisions and explosions". memTip: "Total momentum before = total momentum after. Always." Both contradict keyPoints[1] and spec 4.5.7.2, which apply only "In a closed system".

C6. p5-stopping-distance keyPoints[2].keyTerms "braking force" def: "...transferred from kinetic energy to thermal energy in the brakes". This is wrong physics: a force is not transferred. It is energy that is transferred, by the work the braking force does. (keyTerms is a frozen field.)

C7. p5-forces-motion keyPoints[0].content: "Displacement = 500m at an angle north-east." The direction for 300 m east then 400 m north is about 53 degrees east of north (a bearing of about 037), not north-east (045). "At an angle north-east" is wrong if read as a compass point. [own knowledge]

C8. p5-forces-motion keyPoints[2].memTip: "GA-D: ’Gonna Ace D-grade no more!’". This is a joke phrase with no clear meaning.

C9. p5-momentum checkpoints."2".options[2]: "They absorb kinetic energy and convert it to electrical energy". This distractor is close to absurd.

C10. Overlap across the topic: p5-newtons-laws keyPoints teach mass vs weight, W = mg and the same 70 kg Earth/Moon/deep-space example as p5-gravity keyPoints[0] and its table. This content sits in two lessons.

C11. p5-stopping-distance keyPoints[1].list "Alcohol ... the biggest single impairment" and "Age". Neither claim is in spec 4.5.6.3.2, which names tiredness, drugs, alcohol and distractions. This is a fact added beyond the spec. [own knowledge on the ranking]
