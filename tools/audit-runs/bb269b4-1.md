### F1 (HIGH): p3-changes-of-state says energy "overcomes the forces" in every change of state, including freezing and condensing
- File: lessons/physics/p3-changes-of-state.json
- Field paths and text (old = new; the base already had these and the change kept them):

  - keyPoints[0].writeBullets[1]: "Energy during a change of state → overcomes forces between particles → increases potential energy"
  - keyPoints[0].memTip: "Latent = hidden. ... It is overcoming the forces between particles, not heating them up."
  - commonMistakes[0]: "Thinking temperature rises during a change of state. It stays constant while the energy overcomes the forces between particles. Potential energy increases, but kinetic energy does not."
  - revisionCardBullets[2]: "Energy during a change of state → overcomes forces between particles → increases potential energy (not kinetic)"
- Fault: factual error and contradiction. When a substance freezes or condenses, energy goes out, not in. Its particles lose potential energy. These lines state the opposite for every change of state.
  - Same lesson: keyPoints[0].content says that on a cooling curve "Latent heat is released". writeBullets[7] says "Flat = condensing or freezing, latent heat released". commonMistakes[3] says "condensation and freezing RELEASE latent heat".

- Evidence: P3-TRIAGE.md R3 fact0, fact1 and fact2 accepted exactly this fault and fixed it, but only in p3-particle-model ("When a substance melts or boils, ..."). The same wording in p3-changes-of-state was left unchanged. Spec 4.3.2.3: "the energy supplied changes the energy stored (internal energy)". This sentence covers energy supplied only.
