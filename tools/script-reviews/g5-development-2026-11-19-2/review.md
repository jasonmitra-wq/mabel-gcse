## Overall

Not quite as it stands, though it is close: the content is mostly accurate, the steps are short, and the don't-know replies generally get easier. Before she uses it I would fix the 'all income is counted' reply at 1.11, the birth-rate limitation at 1.12 that comes before birth rate is a measure, the muddled 'interest' line at 5.3, the context-free judgement question at 6.3, and the questions at 5.4 and 5.6c whose answers have just been given. The DTM needs its graph, the Brandt Line picture needs its labels explained, and the classification question needs some income figures to work from. It is also a long run of 'Next' with nothing to do but pick from three options, so I would expect her attention to drift by key point 4.
