## Overall

Not as it stands. It opens by quizzing her on a 'last lesson' she has not had, and leans throughout on terms it never explains (LIC, HIC, GNI, development, infant mortality, the Commonwealth, microfinance, debt relief). The 'changing economy' section never says what the economy changed from and does not tie its percentages to the sectors it has just taught. Several questions are answered by the message just before them, the own-words feedback reads as a mark scheme, and there is no map and very little to do but tap Next. The content is accurate and the order is sensible, so I would let her use it once those are fixed.
