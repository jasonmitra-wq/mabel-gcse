## Overall

Not as it is. The content is mostly sound, but the lesson opens by testing a previous lesson she may not have had, asks about 'political context' without teaching the three kinds of context, and counts oil as primary in 2.1 and then as industry in 2.7. The own-words steps mark too narrowly and show her mark-scheme wording as if it were a model answer, several first hints simply hand over the answer, and the right button is never the first one. I would also want a map at the start and fewer long runs of Next before she uses it.
