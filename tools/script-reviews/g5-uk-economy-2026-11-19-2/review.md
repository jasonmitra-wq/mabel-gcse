## Overall

Not as it stands (this is a geography script, and I have reviewed all six key points as she would meet them). The content is mostly accurate and the terms are usually defined before use, but the multiple-choice questions can nearly all be answered by picking the longest, only sensible option, and several 'don't know' hints ask a question the buttons do not answer. The own-words feedback in key points 3, 4 and 5 shows her the marking notes instead of a model line, and 6.6 asks about a 'political link' that was never explained. With no map or picture and long runs of 'Next', I would want those fixed before she uses it.
