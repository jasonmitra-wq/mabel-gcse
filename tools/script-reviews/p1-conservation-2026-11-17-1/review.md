## Overall

Not as it is. The physics is mostly sound and the language is plain, but five of the questions (1.4's hint, 1.7, 2.6, 3.5, 4.8) can be answered by repeating the bubble just before, so she can get through without thinking. Several things would trip a first-time learner: an unexplained Ek calculation at the start that is never used again, 'wrap hot water in each material', 'two houses' with three options, particles being 'warmed', and the unreadable own-words feedback in 3.6. I would also want something for her to do (a bouncing-ball or insulation-test widget), a question on the variables, and a quiz that covers conservation rather than asking about wasted energy twice.
