## Overall

Not as it stands. The structure is sound and most of the physics follows the specification wording, but too many questions simply ask back the example given a bubble or two earlier (bow, petrol, battery to lamp, spread thinly), so she can get them right without thinking. Three of the eight stores and the heating pathway are never tested, and there are points that would mislead her: 'No force pushes anything along', uranium 'fuel' straight after fuels were called chemical, sound tacked onto radiation, and the battery question that needs light ending up as heat, which she has not been told. I would also want the long runs of Next broken up with something to do, and the 'destroyed' distractor and 'thermal store of the surroundings' repetition cut back, before she uses it.
