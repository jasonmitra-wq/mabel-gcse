## Overall

Not as it stands, though it is close. Most of the physics is sound and the questions use good misconception distractors, but I would fix three things before she uses it: sound is left without a pathway and radiation is not separated from heating (2.5, 2.5b); the slide question credits the kinetic store when the energy comes from height (2.9b); and several questions (1.18, 3.4) need ideas the lesson has not yet taught. The nuclear and electrostatic messages pile up new terms, three of the eight stores are never checked, and the final quiz question contradicts the exam sentence taught at 2.7. With no hands-on activity and no pictures until key point 4, I would also expect her to lose interest in the long runs of Next.
