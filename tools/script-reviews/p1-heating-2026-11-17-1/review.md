## Overall

No, not as it is. The core of key point 1 (steps 1.2 to 1.10) is clear and the numbers are right throughout, but the lesson opens with two questions on energy stores and E = P × t that a first-time learner cannot answer. Rearranging the equation is hardly taught before she is asked to do it, the cooling example uses 60 °C without explaining why it is not −60, and the 'why wait' question in the practical gives a doubtful reason and contradicts the method just given. I would also want a diagram of the practical, something hands-on, and hints that fit the buttons shown before she uses it.
