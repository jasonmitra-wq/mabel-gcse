## Overall

The physics and every number in the script are correct, and the equations are stated with their symbols and units before they are used, so nothing here would teach her something wrong. I would not let her use it quite as it is. The opening assumes a lesson she may not have had, the thermal store and air resistance's effect on energy are used without being taught, and a few lines are muddled (the 2 × 9 step on the first card, 'Speed is different', the missing rounding instruction at 3.5). My bigger worry is that it is one calculation after another with no picture and nothing to try, so I would fix the points above and add something hands-on before she uses it.
