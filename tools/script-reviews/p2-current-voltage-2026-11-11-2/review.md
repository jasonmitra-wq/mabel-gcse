## Overall

The physics is sound and the order is sensible, so I would let her use it, but not quite as it is. The main weakness is the predict-first boxes: in every one the reveal states the answer and the next line asks her to write it, so she copies rather than explains. "Add a cell" in key point 1 also spoils the key point 2 prediction, and the ammeter-connection question is answered by the picture before it. I would fix those, add a worked example for R = V ÷ I with easier numbers, show the voltmeter picture at 2.6, and give the lesson a proper ending.
