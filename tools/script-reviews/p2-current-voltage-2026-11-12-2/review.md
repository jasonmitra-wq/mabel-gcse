## Overall

Key points 1 and 2 are close: the physics is sound, the predict-first circuits are well ordered, and the equations are stated with symbols and units before they are used. Before she uses them I would fix the cell/battery link in 1.3 and the 1.5 V question in 2.5b, which asks her to add potential differences she has never been told add. I would not let her use key points 3 to 7 as they stand. 5.4 contradicts the question before it, 5.6 and 5.13 ask for answers just given, 5.9 asks her to explain a diode before she is told what it does, key point 3 leaves out the resistor combinations, and "charge flows through a potential difference" is loose physics. The two practicals are also long runs of telling with nothing to do, and I would expect her to lose interest there.
