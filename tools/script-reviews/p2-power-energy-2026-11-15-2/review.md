## Overall

Not quite as it is, though it is close: the calculations, worked examples and wrong-answer replies are mostly clear and correct. Before she uses it I would fix the hands-on circuit at 1.10, which asks her to explain using potential difference and current that it neither shows nor has yet taught and carries stray charge readings, and the context-free 'Here is the reason' at 1.9 that follows it. I would also fix the 'how fast the charge moves' hint at 1.1b, the muddled reply to 3600 at 2.7, and 'You also met E = Q V' at 3.2, which is new to her. Key point 3 and the quiz are a long run of typed calculations with little checking of the ideas, so I would also expect her attention to drop there.
