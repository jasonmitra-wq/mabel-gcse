## Overall

No, not as it is. The physics is essentially sound and the wrong-answer replies mostly tackle the right misconceptions, but it opens by quizzing a first-time learner on current, potential difference and V = I R before teaching anything, and it leans on "the last lesson" again for the meters. Too many questions are answered by the message or picture caption just before them, several hints and replies do not fit the buttons or the answer given (4.6 and 3.6 especially), and terms such as branch, resistor and variable resistor are never explained. With no hands-on circuit and only two pictures in 58 steps, I would want these fixed before she uses it.
