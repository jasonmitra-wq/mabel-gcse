## Overall

Not quite as it is, though it is close: the physics is sound, the numbers all check, and the sequence from the meaning of density through calculations, the practical and particles is sensible. Before she uses it I would fix the unit-conversion step at 2.7, which jumps to its conclusion, and the 2.8 question, whose answer has already been given. I would also fix the untaught ideas that appear in feedback or questions (the ice exception at 1.10, eye level at 3.10, callipers and micrometer at 3.3b) and finish the liquid method at 3.9. The lesson also needs something hands-on and a second try after a wrong numeric answer; at present it has a long run of Next taps early on and tells her the answer the moment she slips.
