## Overall

Not as it stands. The physics is mostly sound and the wrong-answer replies are generally helpful, but too many questions are asked straight after the message that gives the answer (2.8, 2.9, 3.10, 3.12, 4.6), so she can score well without understanding. The opening quizzes her on energy stores she has not been taught and then tells her hot tea is not about kinetic energy, which the lesson later contradicts; 'pulls the particles apart' for melting clashes with the lesson's own picture of a liquid. Gas pressure and expansion on heating are never actually taught, 4.7 shows her the marking rubric as feedback, and there are long runs of Next with nothing hands-on. I would want these fixed before she uses it.
