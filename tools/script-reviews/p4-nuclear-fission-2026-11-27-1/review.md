## Overall

Not as it is. The physics is mostly sound, the order is sensible and there is a summary at the end, but nearly every question can be answered by copying the message just before it, and the right answer sits on the middle button throughout the quiz, so she could finish with full marks having learned little. Three places would confuse a first-time learner: neutrons 'absorbed' to stop fission when absorbing a neutron was what caused it (2.7), the fusion key point opening with 'Here, light means...' out of nowhere (3.1), and mass 'may be turned into the energy of radiation' with no explanation (3.3). I would fix those, move the questions away from their answers, tidy the own-words feedback and add something hands-on for the chain reaction before letting her use it.
