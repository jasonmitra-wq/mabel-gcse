## Overall

Not quite as it is. The lesson is pitched well for a first-time learner, with short messages, varied don't-know replies and sound physics in most places, but the heart of key point 2 has a logic gap: she is told the plum pudding model also predicts 'nearly all straight through', then asked to conclude 'mostly empty space' from that same result, and 'only something tiny and very dense could do that' is asserted with no reason. I would fix 2.6 and 2.8, reword the 2.10 options, add a plum pudding picture, change the sodium ion example and tidy the fragment feedback at 3.18 before she uses it. The summary is thin and there is nothing hands-on, so I would also want an interactive scattering activity to hold her interest.
