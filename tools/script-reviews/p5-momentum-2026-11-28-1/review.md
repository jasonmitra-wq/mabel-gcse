## Findings

- **1.1b** [new, minor] "Second: as a car brakes to a stop, where does its kinetic energy go?" — This recall question is not needed anywhere in the lesson and is never referred to again. Opening a momentum lesson with kinetic energy invites her to mix the two up, and the lesson never says how they differ.
- **1.3** [P6, minor] "Mass × velocity is called momentum. The equation is p = m v. Here p is the momentum in kilogram metres per second (kg m/s), m is the mass in kilograms (kg) and v is the velocity in m/s." — The new word, the equation, three symbols and a brand-new compound unit all arrive in one bubble. The definition should come first, then the equation and units.
- **1.3** [P4, minor] "Here p is the momentum in kilogram metres per second (kg m/s)" — The key point says momentum can also be measured in N s and that the units are equivalent. The script never mentions N s, yet key point 1 is treated as complete.

- **2.3** [F1, minor] "In a closed system, the total momentum before an event equals the total momentum after it." — This is the fact 2.1 has just told her ("their total momentum does not change, as long as nothing outside pushes on them"). The rule is stated twice in three bubbles.

- **2.6** [F3, minor] "Explosions work the same way." — "Explosion" is used in the physics sense of things pushing apart from rest, but she is not told that. To her an explosion is a bang, and a cannon firing or skaters pushing apart may not seem like one.

## Overall

Not as it is, though key points 1 and 2 are close: the physics is sound and the worked examples and practice follow sensibly. Key point 3 needs rewriting before she sees it, because Δ is never explained, the derivation in 3.2 is crammed into one bubble with t and Δt mixed, "rate of change" is never explained, and the same safety explanation is told and then asked for three times in a row. The third summary point is a pasted helmet answer beginning "It", and the model lines for the own-words steps are fragments. The lesson also leaves out N s, the collision equation and impulse, never has her use a negative momentum, and springs the grams trap and the recoil calculation on her without a worked example.
