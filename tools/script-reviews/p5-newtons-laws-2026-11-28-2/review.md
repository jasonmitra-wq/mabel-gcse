## Overall

Not quite as it stands. The core of key points 1 to 3 is sound physics in short, clear messages, and most wrong-answer replies are helpful. But I would not let her loose on the experiment section (2.9 to 2.10): the pulley, light gates and hanger are unexplained, and the question's answer is given just before it. The end of key point 4 is the other problem: scale drawings and splitting forces are told in words with no picture or question, after a long run of Next-only messages. I would also fix the 1.9 and 1.11 questions and give a worked example for the rearranged equation, and remember that the lesson assumes she already has acceleration.
