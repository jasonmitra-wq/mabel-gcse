Nuclear is inside atoms' nuclei. Electrostatic is between charged objects, like static on a balloon. Magnetic is between magnets.  Energy moves between stores by pathways, and one is mechanical, meaning pushing, pulling or kicking. When you kick a football, what is doing the pushing?
