**Tutor** [complete, point 3]: Irradiation is exposing an object to nuclear radiation, and the object does not become radioactive. If the source is outside, removing or shielding it stops the irradiation. Contamination is radioactive atoms on or in other materials, and the hazard comes from their decay. Alpha is the most dangerous in contamination, because it is highly ionising near body cells.  Ionising radiation damages DNA, which causes mutations, and mutations can cause cancer. A short half-life means high activity, but not for long. A long half-life means lower activity, but a hazard for a very long time.
